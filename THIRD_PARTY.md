# Document Rendering

The document reader uses [mupdf-rs](https://github.com/messense/mupdf-rs),
Rust bindings to [MuPDF](https://mupdf.com/), under AGPL-3.0.
MuPDF is compiled and linked into eNote by the `mupdf-sys` dependency.
Dependency licenses and notices are included in their Cargo source packages;
redistribution must comply with those licenses.
